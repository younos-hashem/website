Malaria kills [over 600,000](https://ourworldindata.org/malaria) people every year, mostly children under 5. This is entirely preventable: cost-effective interventions, like insecticide-treated nets and antimalarial medications, have the capacity to save thousands of lives a year. Anti-malaria interventions are some of the most cost-effective ways to save lives, costing just [$4,000–$6,000 for one life saved](https://www.givewell.org/impact-estimates#Impact_metrics_for_grants_to_GiveWells_top_charities).

Your donation goes to [GiveWell](https://www.givewell.org), which will allocate the money to the most cost-effective charities based on rigorous research. 100% of your donation goes to saving lives.
